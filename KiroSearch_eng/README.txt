That said, these are the steps to exclude Kirosearch from your Windows Defender "suspicios stuff" list. 

1. Open Settings --> Privacy & security.
2. Click Windows Security, then click Open Windows Security.
3. Click Virus & threat protection.
4. Under Virus & threat protection settings, click Manage settings.
5. Scroll down to Exclusions --> Add or remove exclusions.
6. Click Yes on the Windows prompt asking whether to allow changes.
7. Click Add an exclusion and select File, then choose the KiroSearch program (the .exe) you want to exclude.
